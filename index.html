    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 22 October 2026

    Who we are

    Sau Solution (“we”, “us”) is a local-services startup in Hyderabad, India. We connect customers with verified local vendors and skilled people.

    What we collect

    • From customers: your name, phone number, area, the service you need, your preferred time and a description of the problem.
    • From vendors: your name, phone number, skill or shop type, locality, and later the ID and work details we need to verify you.

    Why we use it

    We use your details only to arrange your request, match you with a vendor, verify vendors, contact you about your request, tell you when our app launches, and improve our service.

    Who we share it with

    We share only what is needed with the vendor matched to your job (your name, phone number, area and job details). We do not sell your personal data. When you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own terms also apply.

    How long we keep it

    We keep your details only as long as needed for the purposes above, and then delete them.

    Your choices

    You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent at any time. Message or call us on +91 99631 82721 and we will respond within a reasonable time. Withdrawing is as easy as agreeing.

    Children

    Our service is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

    Security and changes

    We take reasonable steps to protect your data. If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page.

    Terms of Use

    Last updated: 22 October 2026

    What Sau Solution does

    We help connect customers with independent local vendors and skilled people. Vendors are not our employees. They run their own work and are responsible for it.

    Verification

    We check each vendor’s identity, phone number and skills before listing them. We take care in doing this, but we cannot guarantee the result of every job. Please check the work before you pay.

    Requests and bookings

    A request is not a confirmed booking. A booking is confirmed only when our team confirms the vendor, price and time with you.

    Prices and payment

    The price is agreed between you and the vendor. We currently charge customers no extra fee. If we introduce a fee, we will tell you before you book. Pay the vendor directly unless we tell you otherwise.

    Customers

    Give accurate details, give safe access to the place of work, and treat vendors with respect.

    Vendors

    Give true information and documents, do honest work at fair prices, and be polite and on time. We may remove vendors who break these terms.

    Complaints

    If something goes wrong, contact us on +91 99631 82721. We will help resolve it but cannot promise a particular outcome.

    Limits and law

    To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for indirect losses, or for a vendor’s work. These terms follow the laws of India, and the courts in Hyderabad, Telangana have jurisdiction.
