    Frequently asked questions

    Clear answers to common questions.

    How can I learn about a provider?

    Contact our team to discuss the information available for a provider. Before proceeding, confirm the service details, price and schedule with the provider.

    What does it cost?

    It is free to request. You pay only the vendor’s agreed price. We currently charge customers no extra fee.

    When will someone contact me?

    Our team reviews each request and aims to respond the same day by phone or WhatsApp.

    Is the app available?

    Not yet. For now our team arranges every booking by phone and WhatsApp. Leave your number to hear when the app launches.

    Which areas do you serve?

    We currently serve Hyderabad. Availability may vary by locality and service.

    What if I have a concern about the service?

    Contact us as soon as possible. We will review the concern and speak with the vendor to help address it; we cannot guarantee a particular outcome.

    How can I register as a vendor?

    Complete the vendor interest form on this page. Our team will contact you to discuss your details and next steps.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 22 October 2026

    What we collect

    • From customers: your name, phone number, area, the service you need, your preferred date and time, and a description of the problem.
    • From vendors: your name, phone number, skill or business type, and locality, as submitted through the registration form.

    Why we use it

    We use your details to respond to service requests and vendor registration enquiries, coordinate customer requests with providers, contact you about your request, share app launch updates when requested, and improve our service.

    How requests are sent

    When you submit a service request or vendor enquiry, the website prepares a message with the details you entered and shows you a link to WhatsApp. Review the message and choose whether to send it there. WhatsApp then processes the message under its own terms and privacy policy.

    This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font stylesheet and font files from Google when displaying the page.

    What SAU Solution does

    SAU Solution accepts customer service requests and vendor registration enquiries. Submitting a request does not confirm a booking, and submitting a vendor enquiry does not confirm a partnership.

    Before arranging a service

    Discuss the provider’s qualifications and service details. Agree on the work, price and timing before proceeding.

    Requests and bookings

    A service request sent through this website is an enquiry, not a confirmed booking. Provider availability, price and timing must be discussed and agreed before work begins.

    Prices and payment

    The price is agreed between you and the vendor. We currently charge customers no extra fee. If we introduce a fee, we will tell you before you book. Pay the vendor directly unless we tell you otherwise.
